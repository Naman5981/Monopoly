## 🌟 Executive Summary

Version 2.2.0 introduces targeted enhancements to the visual fidelity of the 3D diorama board, expands camera projection geometry for accurate edge-on alignment, and streamlines gameplay pacing with automated turn progression and differentiated START bonuses.

---

## 🎨 1. UI & Visual Enhancements

### 💡 4-Corner Symmetrical Street Lamps
- **Prompt Requirement:** Street lamps added on all 4 corners of the board.
- **Architectural Update (`src/three/core/lighting.js`):**
  - Expanded `lampPositions` to include full 4-corner symmetry on both the inner sidewalk promenade (`x: ±8.5, z: ±8.5`) and the outer diorama corner brackets (`x: ±14.2, z: ±14.2`).
  - Street lamps are positioned and oriented diagonally at 45-degree angles to illuminate all 4 key corner plazas: **START**, **Island / Jail**, **Vacation / Festival**, and **World Tour**.
  - All lamps reuse existing low-poly geometries and shared materials, maintaining optimal 60 FPS performance without adding draw call or memory overhead.

### 📐 45-Degree Edge-on Rotation & True Square Board Perspective
- **Prompt Requirement:** Make the board look square when moved by 45 degrees such that an edge is at the bottom.
- **Root Cause & Geometry Resolution (`src/three/core/camera.js`, `index.html`):**
  - Previously, rotate buttons only stepped by 90° (`Math.PI / 2`), locking the camera to corner-on diamond angles (45°, 135°, 225°, 315°).
  - Rotation buttons in `camera.js` now increment/decrement by 45° (`Math.PI / 4`), enabling smooth alternation between Diamond view and Edge-on view.
  - In an orthographic projection, looking down at a 41.6° pitch angle caused depth foreshortening ($\sin(41.6^\circ) \approx 0.66$), flattening an edge-on board into a compressed rectangle.
  - Implemented dynamic harmonic elevation blending:
    $$\text{edgeFactor} = \cos^2(2 \cdot \alpha)$$
    $$\text{dist} = 34.0 - 16.0 \cdot \text{edgeFactor}$$
    $$\text{elev} = 30.0 + 14.0 \cdot \text{edgeFactor}$$
  - When an edge is at the bottom (`edgeFactor = 1.0`), camera elevation elevates to $\approx 67.8^\circ$ ($\sin(67.8^\circ) = 0.926$). The board displays as an accurate, upright square, while maintaining depth, shadows, and clear visibility of 3D buildings, pawns, cars, and trees.
  - Updated top bar button labels and tooltips to `↶ 45°` and `↷ 45°`.

## 🎲 2. Game Logic & UX Flow Changes

### 🚩 START Tile Bonus: Land (400K) vs Pass (300K)
- **Prompt Requirement:** Landing on START awards 400K, passing awards 300K.
- **Engine Logic (`src/game/engine.js`, `src/game/data.js`):**
  - In `hopPlayerSteps3D`: Passing bonus of +300K is awarded strictly during intermediate steps (`p.position === 0 && s < steps - 1`).
  - In `evaluateTileLanding`: Landing directly on START (`tile.type === 'start'`) awards a **+400K Super Bonus**, accompanied by a floating HUD banner, celebratory audio, speech bubble, and event log entry.
  - In `executeWorldTourFlight`: Intermediate fly-bys award the +300K passing salary; choosing START as destination awards the direct landing +400K bonus.
  - Updated tile definition subtitle in `BOARD_TILES[0]` to `"Pass +300K / Land +400K"`.

### ⏱️ Automated Turn Progression
- **Prompt Requirement:** Automatically pass the turn to the next player instead of waiting for manual click.
- **Engine Logic (`src/game/engine.js`):**
  - Integrated `autoEndTurnTimer` into `concludeActionPhase()`.
  - When a human player's action phase completes (e.g., rolling non-doubles, purchasing or passing on a city, paying rent, spinning chance), the UI sets `gameState.phase = 'END'` and automatically schedules `advanceTurn()` after a 1200ms grace window.
  - Players can still click "End Turn" immediately to skip the 1.2s delay if desired.
  - The timer safely auto-cancels on manual clicks, new game start, or turn handoff.
