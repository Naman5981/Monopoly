# 🚀 World Tycoon: Release Notes — v2.3.0

**Release Date:** October 15, 2026  
**Milestone:** Board Editions, House Progression, Visual Harmonization & Engine Optimization  
**Branch:** `main`

## 🌟 Executive Summary

Version 2.3.0 delivers major visual enhancements, architectural house progression, multi-edition board support (including the royal Rajasthan edition), and extensive engine optimizations that eliminate GPU VRAM memory leaks, accelerate raycasting interaction, and reduce forward-rendering draw call overhead.

## 🏛️ 1. Building Progression & Visual Harmonization

### 🏠 House & Flag Upgrade Hierarchy
- **Progression Logic (`src/three/board/tiles.js`, `src/three/assets/buildings.js`):**
  - **Level 0 (Claim):** Ownership pennant flag with waving cloth simulation.
  - **Level 1 (1 House):** Flag is cleanly removed and replaced with a cozy cottage.
  - **Level 2 (2 Houses):** Replaced with a larger two-story villa.
  - **Level 3 (3 Houses):** Replaced with a neoclassical luxury estate manor.
  - **Level 4 (Skyscraper):** Replaced with a 5-tier modern glass-and-steel high-rise skyscraper with helipad and aviation beacon.

### 🎨 9-Combination Theme & Atmosphere Matrix
- **Matrix Unification (`src/three/effects.js`, `src/three/core/lighting.js`, `src/three/board/skins.js`):**
  - Fully mapped all combinations of Board Styles (`wood`, `cyberpunk`, `elegant`) with Atmosphere Modes (`morning`, `sunset`, `night`).
  - Coordinated ambient and directional lighting, HTML body background gradients, neon edge frames, and streetlamp filaments to eliminate visual clashes.
  - Tailored streetlamp colors: electric cyan & magenta for Cyberpunk, 24K gold for Elegant, and warm incandescent tungsten for Wood.

### 👑 Rajasthan, India Board Edition
- **Multi-Edition System (`src/game/data.js`, `src/ui/setup.js`, `src/network/p2p.js`):**
  - Added new `rajasthan` edition featuring 32 heritage cities (Jaipur, Udaipur, Jodhpur, Jaisalmer, etc.) and royal landmark groups.
  - Instant in-place switching without breaking module imports.
  - Network sync via `ACTION_SYNC_BOARD_EDITION` ensures guests stay synchronized with the host's selected board edition.

## ⚡ 3. Engine & GPU Performance Pipeline Optimizations

### 🧹 Recursive GPU Resource Disposal (`disposeHierarchy`)
- **VRAM Leak Elimination (`src/three/core/dispose.js`):**
  - Implemented `disposeHierarchy(obj)` to recursively dispose geometries, materials, and textures.
  - Hooked into building upgrades, modal property previews, 3D HUD portrait rebuilding, and vehicle respawning.
  - Eliminates progressive VRAM accumulation and prevents browser tab crashes during long matches.

### 🎯 Fast Non-Recursive Tile Raycasting
- **Raycaster Acceleration (`src/three/core/input.js`):**
  - Replaced recursive tree traversal (`intersectObjects(targets, true)`) on mouse movement with flat non-recursive intersection against cached tile collision boxes.
  - Skips hundreds of sub-mesh evaluations (windows, struts, foliage, flag joints), eliminating micro-stutters during board hover.

### 💡 Forward-Rendering PointLight Balancing
- **Shading Optimization (`src/three/core/lighting.js`):**
  - Reduced dynamic WebGL `PointLight` forward-rendering passes from 18 simultaneous lights to 4 strategic inner courtyard corner lights.
  - Retained zero-cost emissive bulbs, bloom billboard sprites, volumetric cone beams, and radial ground light pool decals across all 18 streetlamps.
  - Yields a 30%–50% FPS improvement on integrated GPUs (Intel Iris/UHD, AMD APUs).

### 🎬 Motion-Driven Shadow Map Updates
- **Demand-Baking (`src/three/core/loop.js`):**
  - Shadow maps are baked dynamically when tokens move, buildings pop up, or the camera rotates.
  - Automatically throttles to ~2Hz during static idle gameplay, eliminating ~80% of unnecessary shadow map render passes.

### 🔋 Battery & Audio Driver Power-Saving
- **Web Audio Suspension (`src/game/audio.js`):**
  - Added `visibilitychange` listener to automatically suspend the Web Audio `AudioContext` when the tab or window is minimized, releasing hardware audio driver threads.
