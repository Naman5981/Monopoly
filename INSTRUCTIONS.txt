========================================================================
         WORLD TYCOON : GLOBAL EDITION (3D WEBRTC MULTIPLAYER)
========================================================================

Hey! Welcome to World Tycoon: Global Edition.
You can play 3D Monopoly online with your friend without installing Node.js,
Python, or any complex software. Everything runs serverless!

------------------------------------------------------------------------
 HOW TO PLAY ONLINE WITH YOUR FRIEND:
------------------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 1: BOTH OF YOU RUN THE GAME
* Double-click "WorldTycoon.exe" (or "run.bat").
* A small launcher console opens, and your default web browser (Edge/Chrome/Brave)
  will automatically open the 3D board at http://localhost:3000/.
* Keep the launcher window open while you play!

STEP 2: ONE PLAYER HOSTS (ROOM LEADER)
* On the main setup page, click "🌐 Online WebRTC (P2P)".
* Under "Host Online Room", click "Create Room".
* You will get a 4-letter Room Code (for example: 8492).
* Click "Copy Code" (or copy the code manually) and send it to your friend!

STEP 3: YOUR FRIEND JOINS
* Your friend opens the game on their computer and clicks "🌐 Online WebRTC (P2P)".
* Under "Join Friend's Room", they enter your 4-letter Room Code (e.g. 8492).
* Click "Connect".
* Both of you will see a green connection confirmation!

STEP 4: START PLAYING!
* The Host clicks "ENTER THE BOARD & START GAME".
* The match begins for all connected players simultaneously!
* Roll 3D dice, hop across cities, buy world capitals, upgrade skyscrapers,
  mortgage properties, spin the Chance Wheel, and send live emotes!

------------------------------------------------------------------------
 HOTKEYS IN LAUNCHER WINDOW:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* [B] - Re-open the game in your browser if you accidentally closed the tab.
* [C] - Connect to a friend's Room Code directly.
* [Q] or [ESC] - Quit the game server.

Have fun becoming the ultimate World Tycoon!
